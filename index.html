Colour is the sound

Inspired by Goethe's colour theory and the exhibition Mozart and Goethe: The Quest of Tone-Colours. Goethe saw colour arising where light meets darkness. Warm colours push forward and feel active, cool colours pull back and feel calm. Here warm colours play bright, sharp sounds, cool colours play soft ones, and black, darkness itself, is the bass.
